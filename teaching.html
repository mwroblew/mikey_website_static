In the classroom

My teaching emphasizes clear economic reasoning and the ability to apply it. Historical cases give students a setting in which to examine how incentives and institutions shape economic outcomes.

I encourage students to interpret data, develop arguments, and assess alternative accounts of policy and institutional change.

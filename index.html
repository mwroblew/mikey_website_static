Monetary economics · Political economy · History

Money, institutions,
and political choice.

I am a Post-Doctoral Research Associate at the Free Market Institute at Texas Tech University. I study how political interests shape monetary policy and the institutions that govern it.

My research brings economic theory together with historical evidence to examine central banking, monetary rules, and the distributional consequences of policy.
